## 2. The Hippocampal Topological Template

### Dabaghian et al. (2012, 2014)

**Citation:** Dabaghian, V., Brandt, V.L., & Frank, L.M. (2014). "Reconceiving the hippocampal map as a topological template." eLife.

**Key Finding:**
> "By providing evidence that hippocampal maps have more in common with subway maps than street maps, the work of Dabaghian et al. offers an explanation for previously challenging results."

The hippocampal cognitive map encodes:
- **Connectivity** (are locations A and B linked?)
- **Continuity** (can you move smoothly between them?)
- **Enclosure** (is the space bounded?)
- **Sequence** (temporal order of traversal)

NOT primarily:
- Euclidean distances
- Precise angles
- Metric properties

**IST Connection:** This is exactly the topological encoding that a Möbius substrate would produce. The non-orientable surface preserves connectivity and continuity but does not encode metric geometry — metric properties (distance, angle) must be emergent from the interference of multiple topological threads.

### Curto & Itskov (2008)

**Citation:** Curto, C. & Itskov, V. (2008). "Cell groups reveal structure of stimulus space." PLoS Computational Biology.

**Key Finding:** The simplicial complex formed by place cell coactivity correctly encodes the **topology** of the environment. A population of place cells can learn the topological structure of a new environment in **biologically plausible time** (~25 minutes for a rat).

**IST Prediction:** The time to learn a new topological structure should scale as φ^n where n is the complexity of the environment. Simple environments (few connected components) are learned faster; complex environments (many components, holes, barriers) take φ-times longer per additional feature.

## 3. Neural Manifolds: Geometry of Population Activity

### Grid Cell Torus (2022)

**Finding:** Grid cells in the medial entorhinal cortex form a **toroidal manifold**. The population activity is confined to a 2D torus, with each ring encoding a movement direction.

**IST Connection:** The torus is the orientable double-cover of the Klein bottle. If the grid cell manifold is actually a **Klein bottle** (non-orientable), then the two "movement directions" on the torus are chiral projections of the same underlying substrate. This predicts:

### Grid Cell Scale Ratio

**Finding (Giocomo et al., Stensola et al.):** The scale ratio between successive grid cell modules along the dorso-ventral axis is approximately **√2 ≈ 1.42**.

**IST Hypothesis:** The √2 ratio may be an approximation of φ/1.14, or it may reflect the **two-sided nature** of the Möbius substrate. If the true ratio is φ^(1/2) ≈ 1.27 or φ^(-1/2) ≈ 0.79, experimental measurement of √2 could be a superposition of these two projections.

Alternatively, the ratio could be **2/φ ≈ 1.236** or **φ ≈ 1.618**, and the observed √2 ≈ 1.42 is an intermediate value reflecting the **two-sided Möbius correction**: √(1 + 1/φ⁴) ≈ 1.07 applied to a base ratio.
