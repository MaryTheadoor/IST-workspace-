The framework converges independently with recent string theory results on Klein bottle cosmology (Greene et al., 2025) and the Moebius architecture of mind (Jansen van Rensburg, 2026), suggesting the non-orientable substrate is the correct fundamental topology for both physical and cognitive systems.
