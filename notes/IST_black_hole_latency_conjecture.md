## Conventional anchors (each element of the conjecture maps to a known result)

1. **Density threshold** — the Bekenstein bound S ≤ A/4 *is* a maximum
   information density; collapse occurs when matter entropy would exceed it;
   the BH entropy S = A/4 saturates it. "Threshold creates the horizon" =
   Bekenstein saturation.
2. **Surface encoding** — the holographic principle / BH complementarity:
   the exterior description carries the full information content on the
   horizon area.
3. **Is there an interior?** Classically yes (full interior geometry), but
   causally sealed and observationally inaccessible by construction; modern
   programs (complementarity, fuzzballs, islands) dispute its naive reality.
4. **"Pushed into the future"** — in Schwarzschild coordinates r and t
   *swap roles* inside the horizon: the singularity is not a place but a
   time, unavoidable the way tomorrow is. For external observers the
   collapsing matter never crosses the horizon (infinite latency). Interior
   volume grows linearly with time (Christodoulou–Rovelli): the BH is a
   growing, sealed time-capsule.
5. **Mass as latency** — null-cone tipping ⇒ coordinate signal speed → 0 at
   the horizon; round-trip latency diverges exactly there; evaporation time
   ~ M³ is the longest latency in nature.
6. **Wormhole to a time, not a place** — the interior lies to the *future*
   of every exterior observer; the only "exit" is evaporation, distributed
   over ~ M³. No traversable spatial wormhole is required or implied.
