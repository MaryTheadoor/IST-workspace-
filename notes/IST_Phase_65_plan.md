proposed to be its pre-geometric dual: *elliptic* — a closed cycle (the
Ω/Ω_inv loop of the directed-numbers runtime), orthogonal to every emergent
direction but with the opposite (closed, not open) geometry. This phase makes
the conjecture checkable in the runtime.

**The checkable statement (pre-registered).** The zero-point operators
realize a **closed cycle with unit-modulus return** (elliptic), in precise
contrast to the temporal axis's **open growth with eigenvalue φ** (hyperbolic,
Phase 58's substitution RG). One substrate, two complementary geometries:

- Zero point: Ω then Ω_inv returns the number exactly (the Plan 9
  compression–expansion cycle: 4.76 → 0 → 4.76, information conserved) —
  iterating the cycle stays **bounded**; the return-map eigenvalue is
  **|λ| = 1** (elliptic, closed — a circle).
- Parity: the seam flip is a **period-2** structure (W = −1, W² = +1, θ = ½)
  — the wound meridian circle; flip twice and everything returns.
- Time: the temporal substitution (A→AB, B→A) grows by the eigenvalue
  **φ = 1.618 > 1** (Phase 58, exact) — iterating never returns, amplitudes
  grow as φⁿ (hyperbolic, open — the expansion direction).

  growth eigenvalue is φ > 1 (recomputed; Phase 58's exact result) and

  **confirmed at the runtime level** if the contrast is exact (|λ| = 1 vs
