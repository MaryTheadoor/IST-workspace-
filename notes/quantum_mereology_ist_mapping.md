## 2. The three standard problems, and IST's resources

### 2.1 Mereological harmony ([A] §6.1, [B])

**Problem:** Harmony principles require a part's location to be a subregion of the whole's location — but non-spatiotemporal parts have no spatiotemporal location.

**[B]'s fix:** generalize location to any metric structure (physics already uses location in configuration/phase space), restricting inheritance principles to parts and wholes "within the same structure." [B] then defers: the exact non-spatiotemporal structure is "up to future physics."

**IST's position:** IST *is* a candidate answer to that deferral. The substrate carries an internal non-spatiotemporal metric — graph distance on the discrete lattice plus the seam holonomy as connection. Threads are located *in the substrate*; spacetime location is inherited downward through crystallization. IST doesn't merely evade the harmony objection via Very-WIL — it exhibits the concrete structure [B]'s reformulation requires. Cite [B] as philosophical license for the substrate's internal geometry.

### 2.2 The cardinality gap ([A] §6.2)

**Problem:** a countable discrete fundamental structure cannot mereologically compose an uncountable continuum (power-set counting notwithstanding, the physical point stands).

**Standard escape** (Baron & Le Bihan 2024): continuum points beyond a scale factor are excess mathematical structure.

**IST's position:** takes this escape *with a mechanism attached*. The Phase 58 trace-map RG is precisely how a countable quasiperiodic structure generates continuum behavior as a limit; Phases 13/14 make spacetime dimensionality itself scale-dependent, so "points" are effective bookkeeping. The gap dissolves because the continuum was never claimed to be physically occupied. Additionally: IST's pre-registered anchors (ε₀ = α/φ², Δ₀ = ln φ) live at *discrete* scales — the theory's predictions never require the continuum to be real.

### 2.3 Quantum superposition ([A] §6.3)

**Problem:** mereology presupposes definite states; the fundamental state (LQG weave superposition) is a superposition. Decoherence only approximately suppresses interference.

**IST's position — the strongest of the three:** the zero point *is* the superposition of all potential information. Parthood is emergent: before the coherence threshold there are no parts, only the whole; "having parts" is a property the substrate acquires in the crystallized regime. Other programs treat superposition as an obstacle to mereological models; IST treats it as the **pre-mereological phase**. This reframing — *emergence of mereology, not merely mereological emergence* — is a distinctive, citable contribution that costs nothing: it is already what P0–P4 assert.
